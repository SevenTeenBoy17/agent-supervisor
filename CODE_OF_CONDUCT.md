# Code of conduct

We want collaboration here to be safe, constructive, and technically rigorous.

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas and code rather than people,
respect privacy and consent, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or
sexualized conduct. Security reports must not expose live credentials or private data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently limit
participation when needed to protect the community. Good-faith technical disagreement is
welcome; abusive conduct is not.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will
handle reports as confidentially as practical and will avoid conflicts of interest.
